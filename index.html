How a stitch is born

The embroidery process

Print lays ink on top of fabric. Embroidery rebuilds the design in thread, through the fabric. It's slower and costs more per piece — and it's why this store exists as its own format.

01

Digitizing

The HERSKY lockup is redrawn as a stitch file — every line becomes a thread path. Fine detail that can't hold thread is removed honestly, never faked.

02

Thread match

Colours are matched to real thread: bone white, ink black, thread orange, gold, forest. What you pick is the physical thread that ships.

03

Hoop & stitch

Each garment is hooped and stitched to order by Fourthwall's fulfillment partners — flat stitch on chests, 3D puff available on the hat.

04

Checked & shipped

Fourthwall handles checkout, taxes as merchant of record, customer support, and the quality guarantee — a stitch error or defect is replaced or refunded.

Straight answers

FAQ & policies

Is Drop 01 open for payment right now?

Not yet — and this page won't pretend otherwise. Drop 01 opens on Jake's existing Fourthwall store (account already created, products staged hidden) the moment he publishes it. Until then, reserving by email is free and takes no payment. The full current catalog is live now at hersky.info/shop.

Why is shipping already in the price?

Jake's standing rule for every store he runs: the price you see is the price you pay for the piece, shipping included. No checkout surprises.

Can I get any of the 9,000+ designs embroidered?

No — and that's deliberate. Detailed full-colour artwork can't be honestly stitched; it would come out muddy. Embroidery here is only the clean HERSKY lockups that digitize properly. Everything else stays on the print store, front and back, exactly as previewed there.

What if my stitching is defective?

Quality is guaranteed on Fourthwall orders: a print/stitch error or visible quality issue is replaced or refunded. Because pieces are made to order, general returns and sizing swaps aren't accepted — check the size notes before ordering.
